# 🏠 Smart Home Air Quality Monitor

## 📌 Problem Statement

A family in Milan, Europe, is concerned about indoor air quality during winter when wood is burned for heating. This project designs an IoT-based Smart Home Air Quality Monitoring System using an ESP32 that continuously monitors smoke/gas concentration, temperature, and humidity. The system provides local visual and audible alerts based on air quality levels and continues to function even without an internet connection.

---

# 💡 Solution Approach

The system uses an **MQ-2 gas sensor** to detect smoke and gas concentration and a **DHT22 (used in Wokwi; replace with DHT11 for physical hardware)** to measure temperature and humidity.

When the system starts, it first calibrates the MQ-2 sensor by storing a baseline gas value. During operation, the current gas reading is continuously compared with this baseline to determine the air quality level. Based on the measured value, the air quality is classified into three categories:

- **SAFE** – Normal indoor air quality
- **MODERATE** – Increased smoke levels detected
- **DANGER** – Hazardous smoke concentration

According to the detected air quality:

- 🟢 Green LED lights up when the air quality is safe.
- 🟡 Yellow LED indicates moderate smoke levels.
- 🔴 Red LED warns of dangerous air quality.
- The buzzer generates different alert tones for moderate and danger conditions.

All sensor readings are displayed on the Serial Monitor in CSV format, making the data simple to save or analyze later. For a real ESP32 setup, Bluetooth notifications can also be sent to a paired smartphone using the built-in BluetoothSerial library. Since Bluetooth communication is not supported in Wokwi, a simulated mobile notification is provided to demonstrate the expected alerts.

# ⚙️ Features

- Real-time smoke and gas monitoring
- Temperature and humidity measurement
- Automatic calibration of the MQ-2 sensor
- Three-level air quality detection
- RGB LED status indication
- Multi-level buzzer alerts
- CSV data logging through the Serial Monitor
- Bluetooth notification support on a physical ESP32
- Works without an internet connection

---

# 📊 Air Quality Thresholds

| Gas Difference from Baseline | Status |
|-----------------------------:|--------|
| Less than 80 | 🟢 SAFE |
| 80 – 249 | 🟡 MODERATE |
| 250 and above | 🔴 DANGER |
